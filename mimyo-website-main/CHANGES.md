# Mimyo website v3: changes from the approved design

*2 Nov 2026. Baseline: the approved Figma exports (Homepage, HCP page, Contact page). Everything not listed here is unchanged from the approved design. Codes refer to LHP's feedback of 27 Oct and the decisions on it (`feedback_2026-10-27/`).*

## Across the site
- **Nav:** "Science" becomes **"Healthcare professionals"** so the pro area is easy to find (P1). "Get in touch" opens the contact page.
- **3D device:** it appears only once, in the "Inside Mimyo" walkthrough on the home page. It no longer travels through the other sections.
